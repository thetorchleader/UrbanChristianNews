By God's grace, Gospel Light Society International announces that Letters to Young Black Men: The Complete 20th Anniversary Edition — Daniel Whyte III's Essence Magazine national bestseller — is now available straight from BCNN1, for a Black Friday love offering of $5 or more ($10 suggested).

Twenty years ago, Letters to Young Black Men went out across this nation and took its place in homes, churches, schools, and prisons — a father's letters, written to mentor a generation of young men in the faith, in education, and in manhood. The complete book holds twenty-five letters. This is the 20th Anniversary Edition: the Foreword by his sons, Daniel Whyte IV and Danyel Ezekiel Whyte; both Introductions; the endorsements and letters from readers; the four quote pages; and all twenty-five letters complete, with the closing pages — 90 pages in all.
